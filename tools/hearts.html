Heart words

Every sight word, in the Word pop level it's taught in. Red, underlined letters are tricky: they don't make the sounds the game has taught (the word is irregular, like said, or the pattern isn't taught yet, like high). The game marks those letters and shows a ♥. Green words have no tricky letters: the child can sound them out.

A word goes in the level of the stage that teaches its last regular sound, most common words first (25 words in the first two levels, 50 after). The 14 most common words come early anyway (the, you, was, said, he, we, my; then she, they, are, for, have, what, be): until their sounds are taught, those letters show as tricky too. To change a marking, tell Claude the word and which letters should be tricky; it lives in HEART_MARKS in content.js.
