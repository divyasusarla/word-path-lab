Before you start

Find a quiet room with soft things around (curtains, a sofa, a rug) to cut echo. Sit about a hand's width from your laptop, with the microphone near the keyboard.

Each sound shows how to say it. Record, listen back, and redo it if it's not right. Your recordings are kept in this browser as you go, so you can stop and come back later. When you're done, press Download all.

Your browser will ask to use the microphone. Choose Allow. On a Mac, if nothing is heard, check System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and make sure your browser is switched on.
